# Adversarial Econometric Review

Act as an adversarial asset-pricing referee. Assume the numerical output is accurate and try to show that the interpretation does not follow.

Review

1. whether the short-rate innovation is a valid state-variable innovation;
2. whether beta dispersion is sufficient to identify the risk price;
3. whether the two-pass inference is valid under weak or irrelevant factors;
4. whether high cross-sectional R-squared is mechanically induced by the test-asset structure;
5. whether joint anomaly tests truly impose one common price of risk;
6. whether comparator models use identical assets and dates;
7. whether the post-2013 extension is genuinely out of sample;
8. whether monetary regimes were declared before results;
9. whether short regimes have enough observations;
10. whether structural-break tests account for multiple search;
11. whether high-frequency policy and information shocks are correctly identified and aggregated;
12. whether any causal language exceeds the design;
13. whether data revisions or unavailable portfolios undermine claims of replication;
14. whether economic significance survives uncertainty and transaction or implementation considerations where relevant.

For each finding state the claim threatened, the mathematical or econometric reason, the decisive diagnostic, and a repair. Finish by classifying the contribution as publishable, potentially publishable after major revision, or not identified.
